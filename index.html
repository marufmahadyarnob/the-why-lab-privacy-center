Privacy Center

Your privacy matters. Learn how we handle information and protect your trust.

The Why Lab is an educational content platform dedicated to explaining science, mathematics, technology, everyday phenomena, and interesting questions in simple and engaging ways. This page outlines how we handle information across our website and official social channels.

Effective Date: November 14, 2026 · Last Updated: November 14, 2026

Privacy at a Glance

A concise summary of our core principles before you read the full policy.

We do not sell personal information

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

No sign-up required for public content

      01Introduction

      Welcome to The Why Lab.

      The Why Lab is an educational content platform dedicated to exploring interesting questions, explaining scientific concepts, sharing knowledge, and making learning simple and engaging.

      We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you visit our website, interact with our social media pages, or communicate with us.

      By accessing our content or communicating with us, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

      02Scope of This Policy

      This Privacy Policy applies to information received or handled by The Why Lab in connection with:

      • Our static website and Privacy Center hosted on GitHub Pages.
      • Direct communications sent to us by email or direct messages.
      • Interactions on our official social media pages on Facebook, Instagram, and YouTube to the extent those interactions are visible to or managed by us.

      This policy distinguishes between information directly received and controlled by The Why Lab and data independently collected or processed by external platforms under their own privacy policies.

      03Information We Collect

      We do not require visitors to provide personal information to access our public educational content.

      However, we may receive certain information when you voluntarily interact with us, including:

      • Your name or display name.
      • Your email address.
      • Your social media username.
      • Messages, comments, questions, and feedback.
      • Any other information you voluntarily provide when contacting us.

      04Information Collected Automatically

      We may receive limited technical information through our website hosting provider or third-party platforms, depending on the services enabled.

      For example, because this website is hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may automatically log technical connection details—such as your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URL, and access timestamps—for security, reliability, and service operation under GitHub's own privacy practices.

      The Why Lab does not run a custom backend database or user tracking system on this static site.

      05How We Use Your Information

      We may use the information you voluntarily provide for the following purposes:

      • To respond to questions, messages, and inquiries.
      • To communicate with our audience.
      • To improve the quality of our educational content.
      • To understand audience feedback and engagement.
      • To maintain and improve our website and social media presence.
      • To prevent spam, abuse, and unauthorized activities.
      • To maintain the security and proper operation of our services.

      We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

      06Comments, Messages, and Public Interactions

      When you comment on our educational posts, react to content, or message our pages, information you share in public areas may be visible to us and to other users.

      Please keep in mind that content posted publicly on social media or public comment sections can be viewed, copied, or shared by others. We encourage you not to include sensitive personal details in public comments.

      07Cookies and Similar Technologies

      Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to improve user experience, maintain essential functionality, and understand website performance.

      Cookies are small text files stored on your device by websites. You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings; however, disabling certain browser storage features may affect some website functionality.

      On this Privacy Center site, we use browser localStorage strictly for non-sensitive interface preferences such as your chosen language (English or Bengali) and display theme (Light or Dark mode). The use of any additional analytics or tracking technologies depends on the services actually implemented on our website or third-party platforms.

      08Third-Party Services and Links

      The Why Lab may use or link to third-party platforms and services, including:

      • Facebook
      • Instagram
      • YouTube
      • Google (for example, web typography delivery via Google Fonts)
      • GitHub (including GitHub Pages for static website hosting)

      Our content may also include links to external websites, educational resources, or other online services. These third-party websites and services operate independently and maintain their own privacy policies and security practices.

      We do not control how third-party services collect, process, store, or use personal information, and we are not responsible for their content or privacy practices. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

      09Social Media Platforms

      When you interact with The Why Lab on Facebook, Instagram, or YouTube, certain information may become visible to us or other users, including:

      • Public profile information.
      • Comments and reactions.
      • Messages sent to our pages.
      • Content shared or posted publicly.
      • Aggregated engagement and audience statistics provided by the platform.

      Your interactions on these platforms are independently governed by the terms and privacy policies of Facebook, Instagram, and YouTube, which operate separately from The Why Lab.

      10How We Share Information

      We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

      However, information may be disclosed in limited circumstances:

      • When you provide permission.
      • When disclosure is necessary to operate or maintain our services through relevant service providers.
      • When required by applicable laws or legal processes.
      • When necessary to protect our rights, security, or the safety of others.
      • When investigating spam, fraudulent activities, or misuse of our services.

      We aim to limit any disclosure to what is reasonably necessary.

      11Data Retention

      We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy. The retention period may depend on:

      • The nature of the information.
      • The purpose for which it was provided.
      • The need to respond to inquiries.
      • Applicable legal requirements.

      When information is no longer necessary, we will take reasonable steps to delete it or stop using it where practical. Information retained by third-party platforms is subject to their respective retention policies.

      12Data Security

      We take reasonable measures to protect information that is under our control, including appropriate access restrictions and reasonable security practices.

      However, no method of electronic storage, internet transmission, or online communication is completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted or stored online.

      13Children's Privacy

      The Why Lab publishes educational content intended for a general audience, including students and young learners.

      We do not knowingly request or intentionally collect personal information directly from children through our website. We encourage parents and guardians to supervise children's online activities and guide them when using social media platforms.

      If a parent or guardian believes that a child has provided personal information directly to us, they may contact us so that we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

      14Your Privacy Rights

      Depending on applicable laws and circumstances, you may contact us to:

      • Request information about personal data you have shared with us.
      • Request correction of inaccurate information.
      • Request deletion of information you voluntarily provided.
      • Ask us to stop using your information for a particular purpose.
      • Withdraw consent where applicable.

      We will review reasonable requests and respond appropriately. Please note that certain information may remain stored by third-party services, which operate independently of The Why Lab.

      15International Data Processing

      Some third-party services used to access or interact with our content may process information on servers located in different countries.

      The handling of such information may be subject to the laws and regulations of those countries. We do not control the independent data processing activities of third-party platforms.

      16Legal Basis and Applicable Law

      Where applicable, we rely on straightforward, practical grounds when handling information you voluntarily share with us—such as your consent when you choose to contact us, our legitimate need to answer inquiries, improve educational materials, and prevent spam or abuse, and compliance with applicable legal obligations.

      Your rights and our obligations may vary depending on your jurisdiction and applicable local data protection laws.

      17Changes to This Privacy Policy

      We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, website functionality, or applicable requirements.

      Any updates will be published on this page with a revised Last Updated date. We encourage visitors to review this page periodically.
